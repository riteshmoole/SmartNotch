HOW TO OPEN SMARTNOTCH (one-time, about 30 seconds)

SmartNotch is free and isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate,
so macOS blocks it the first time. This is expected.

1. Drag SmartNotch into the Applications folder (in this window).
2. Open SmartNotch from Applications. macOS says it "can't be opened" / "Apple could
   not verify…". Click "Done" (do NOT move it to the Trash).
3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the "Security"
   section. You'll see "SmartNotch was blocked…". Click "Open Anyway".
4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click "Open Anyway" again.

After that it opens normally. Look for the notch at the top of your screen and
the SmartNotch icon in the menu bar.

Updating later: when a new version is out, a red dot appears on the gear in the
open notch. Click it, download, quit SmartNotch, then drag the new app into
Applications and choose Replace. Your settings carry over.

Requirements: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
