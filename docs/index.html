Your notch,
but useful.

SmartNotch turns the MacBook notch into a Dynamic Island-style hub with music controls, a file shelf, clipboard history and quick tools. Free, private and open source.

Download for Mac Free · Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) · macOS 14+ · How to open it

Everything, one hover away

Hover over the notch, click it, or press ⌃ ⌥ Space. Move away and it tucks itself back in.

🎵

Now Playing

Artwork, scrubbing, play/pause/skip and volume for Music, Spotify and video in your browser.

📥

File Shelf

Drag files onto the notch to park them. Drag them out to any app or folder, or AirDrop them.

📋

Clipboard history

Your recent copies, text and images. Passwords from password managers are skipped automatically.

⚡️

Quick tools

Keep Awake, Focus, Mirror and one-tap timers sit right under the player. CPU, memory and Wi-Fi are one tab over.

🪞

Mirror

A quick camera check before a call. It turns off the moment the notch closes.

🎨

Themes

Liquid Glass Light and Dark for Apple's glass look (slide from clear to frosted), five solid looks with optional glow, or make your own with a tiny JSON file.

Live activities beside the notch

Even when it's closed, the notch shows what's happening: music playing, a timer counting down, your volume, or a quick charging animation when you plug in or unplug.

Install in a minute

SmartNotch is a free student project, so it isn't registered with Apple's paid developer program. macOS asks you to confirm once. Here's how:

  1. Download SmartNotch.zip. Safari unzips it for you. In other browsers, double-click the ZIP in Downloads.
  2. Drag SmartNotch from Downloads into Applications.
  3. Open SmartNotch from Applications. macOS says it can't verify the app. Click Done. Don't move it to the Trash.
  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click Open Anyway next to "SmartNotch was blocked". Confirm with Touch ID or your password.

That's it. After that it opens like any other app. Look for the icon in your menu bar. The full source code is on GitHub if you'd like to check it first.

Updates find you

When a new version is out, a red dot appears on the gear in the open notch. Click it to see what's new and download it. The menu bar icon gets an Update Available item too.

A new version is ready

To update, quit SmartNotch, drag the new version into Applications and choose Replace. Your settings and permissions carry over.

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing about you leaves your Mac. The only network request is a daily check for new versions, and you can turn it off. SmartNotch only asks for a permission when you use the feature that needs it.

Questions

Does it work on my Mac?

Any Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Best on MacBooks with a notch. Other screens get a floating pill.

macOS says "SmartNotch.dmg" Not Opened. What now?

That's the old download format. macOS blocks it before it even opens. Download the ZIP instead and follow the steps above. If you'd rather keep the DMG: click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway, once for the DMG and once more for the app.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Apple's warning appears for any app that isn't notarized through its $99/year developer program. SmartNotch is free and open source, and you confirm it once in Privacy & Security.

Will Now Playing always work?

It uses a workaround to read what's playing, and a future macOS update could break it. If that happens, SmartNotch falls back to Music and Spotify and tells you, instead of showing an empty panel.

Can it show my notifications or who's calling?

No. Apple doesn't let apps read other apps' notifications. The "In a call" indicator only knows that your mic or camera is busy, so it appears once you answer, not while the call is ringing.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit it from the menu bar icon and drag SmartNotch from Applications to the Trash. Optionally delete ~/Library/Application Support/SmartNotch.

How do I get updates?

SmartNotch checks for a new version when it starts and once a day. When one is out, a red dot appears on the gear in the open notch and an Update Available item appears in the menu bar. Click either, then Download, quit SmartNotch, and drag the new SmartNotch from Downloads into Applications (choose Replace). The download link always serves the newest version too.
